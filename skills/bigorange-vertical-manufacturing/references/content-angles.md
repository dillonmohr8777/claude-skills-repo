# Manufacturing content angles

15 angles grouped by buyer job. Each has a one-line hook in BigOrange voice and the proof it needs before it can ship. Hooks are drafts, treat the specifics (numbers, names) as placeholders unless already sourced in `industry-brief.md`; never fill a placeholder with an invented stat.

## Job: "Show me you understand my actual problem"

1. **Hook:** Your shop can build almost anything. Your website makes it look like you build nothing in particular.
   **Proof needed:** None required, positioning, not a claim.

2. **Hook:** "Quality, reliability, on-time delivery" is on every manufacturer's homepage. It's also on your competitor's.
   **Proof needed:** No.

3. **Hook:** Your capabilities live in a PDF nobody downloads. Here's what happens when they live on a page instead.
   **Proof needed:** No, diagnostic framing from "What is usually broken."

## Job: "Prove this isn't just an agency pitch"

4. **Hook:** A manufacturing client closed a $150,000 deal that started with someone finding them online, not at a trade show.
   **Proof needed:** Yes, named BigOrange case; must trace to source_ref before publishing.

5. **Hook:** One client got a sales-qualified lead the day after their new site launched. Here's what was different about it.
   **Proof needed:** Yes, named BigOrange case (AMS); must trace to source_ref.

6. **Hook:** 1.4 million buyers are searching for suppliers on Thomasnet every month. Are you one of the shops they can actually find?
   **Proof needed:** Yes, cite Thomasnet, dated, linked.

## Job: "Help me understand what's actually broken on my site"

7. **Hook:** Five things missing from most manufacturer websites (and a redesign won't fix any of them by itself).
   **Proof needed:** No stat required, grounded in "What is usually broken."

8. **Hook:** An RFQ form with no explanation of what happens next is where good leads go quiet.
   **Proof needed:** No.

9. **Hook:** If you can't tell which trade show or webpage actually produced your last shipped part, your budget is a guess.
   **Proof needed:** No.

## Job: "Help me decide whether marketing is worth the spend"

10. **Hook:** Manufacturing employment dipped and trade uncertainty topped the list of concerns in 2025. Here's why that makes visibility matter more, not less.
    **Proof needed:** Yes, cite Deloitte 2026 Manufacturing Industry Outlook, dated.

11. **Hook:** Trade shows and relationships built your book of business. Here's why the next generation of buyers won't wait for a booth.
    **Proof needed:** No, logical argument grounded in buyer-journey section, not a stat claim by itself.

12. **Hook:** Questions to ask any marketing agency before they touch your capabilities page.
    **Proof needed:** No.

## Job: "Help me understand the AI search and directory shift"

13. **Hook:** Ask ChatGPT which shop can machine your part. If you don't come up, here's what that's costing you.
    **Proof needed:** No stat required for the hook; any specific AI-visibility claim about a prospect must be a live, checkable demo.

14. **Hook:** Getting found on Thomasnet and getting found on Google are two different jobs. Most shops only do one.
    **Proof needed:** No.

## Job: "Show me the people, not just the pitch"

15. **Hook:** Meet the team behind the $150,000 organic deal. (Behind-the-scenes / team-credit post, social-voice format.)
    **Proof needed:** Yes, must trace to source_ref for the specific case; if used purely as team-credit without result specifics, no stat required.
