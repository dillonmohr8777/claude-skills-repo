# Manufacturing pitch deck narrative

Slide-by-slide arc for a manufacturing-vertical pitch deck. Slot names match what `bigorange-client-decks/recipes/industry-pitch.json` expects to fill from `references/industry-brief.md` (this file) and this narrative file. Each slide names its slots, the brief field feeding each slot, BigOrange-voice guidance for the copy, and whether the slide requires a verified proof point before it can ship.

General voice reminder: one playful line per section max, no em dashes, plain verbs, always a human consequence next to any technical claim. See `bigorange-brand-system` voice contract for the full rulebook.

## 1. Title

- **Slots:** title, kicker
- **Brief field:** none (client name/logo comes from the deck request)
- **Voice guidance:** Kicker names the reader's world, "for shops whose website undersells what they can actually build." Title plain, no jargon.
- **Verified proof required:** No.

## 2. The problem, in their words

- **Slots:** kicker, body
- **Brief field:** "Who the buyer is", what they're worried about
- **Voice guidance:** Open with the owner's world, not the pitch, pride in the shop floor, frustration that it doesn't show online. Ordinary language, one line.
- **Verified proof required:** No.

## 3. Your buyers are already searching without you

- **Slots:** kicker, stat, proof
- **Brief field:** "Their customer's journey", Thomasnet's 1.4 million monthly buyer figure
- **Voice guidance:** Let the number carry it. One sentence connecting it to the reader's own missed opportunity. Cite Thomasnet and the date.
- **Verified proof required:** Yes, external, cited stat (Thomasnet, business.thomasnet.com landing page). Source and date must appear on the slide.

## 4. The market you're actually selling into right now

- **Slots:** kicker, stat, body
- **Brief field:** "Seasonality and market context", Deloitte outlook (ISM below 50, trade uncertainty)
- **Voice guidance:** State the headwind honestly, then pivot to why that raises the stakes on being found, not lowers them. No spin, brand voice rule against promising magic applies doubly to macro conditions.
- **Verified proof required:** Yes, Deloitte figures, source and date on slide.

## 5. What's actually broken

- **Slots:** kicker, body (list of 3-4 items)
- **Brief field:** "What is usually broken"
- **Voice guidance:** Name specifics, "capabilities buried in a PDF" beats "poor website content." One line per item.
- **Verified proof required:** No, diagnostic framing; confirm against the actual prospect site before naming their specific gaps.

## 6. Meet the guide, not the hero

- **Slots:** kicker, body
- **Brief field:** none (BigOrange positioning)
- **Voice guidance:** BigOrange as guide, manufacturer as hero. Plain, one sentence, no oversell.
- **Verified proof required:** No.

## 7. How your next customer is actually looking for you

- **Slots:** kicker, body, stat
- **Brief field:** "Their customer's journey", Gartner rep-free research stat, applied to industrial buyers
- **Voice guidance:** Translate the abstract B2B stat into an engineer sourcing a part late on a Thursday. Cite Gartner plainly.
- **Verified proof required:** Yes, Gartner stat (61% rep-free preference), source and date visible.

## 8. What we'll build: the plan

- **Slots:** title, body (numbered steps)
- **Brief field:** none (BigOrange's own service names, content marketing, SEO-optimized website, AI search optimization, StoryBrand messaging, CRM setup, social/paid)
- **Voice guidance:** Plain verbs: find, fix, show, check, build. Concrete steps, not aspiration.
- **Verified proof required:** No, this is the offer, not an external claim.

## 9. Proof it works

- **Slots:** kicker, proof (2-3 named results), stat
- **Brief field:** "Proof patterns that work"
- **Voice guidance:** Named, dated, numbered results only. "$150,000 deal brought in organically" and "a sales-qualified lead the day after launch" are the model sentences, specific dollar figures and specific timeframes.
- **Verified proof required:** Yes, absolutely. Every claim on this slide must trace to a source_ref. Never invent a case study or round a number up. If no verified proof exists for this prospect's specific sub-industry, use BigOrange's own general manufacturing proof and say so, never fabricate a substitute.

## 10. Answering the objection in the room

- **Slots:** kicker, body
- **Brief field:** "Objections and answers" (pick 1-2 most relevant)
- **Voice guidance:** Say the objection first, "you've always sold through trade shows and relationships, so why would this matter now." Then answer plainly.
- **Verified proof required:** Only if the answer cites a stat, then source and date required.

## 11. What we'll measure

- **Slots:** kicker, body (KPI ladder, condensed)
- **Brief field:** "KPIs and measurement ladder"
- **Voice guidance:** "Here's how you'll know it's working." Pair each KPI (RFQ volume, RFQ quality, revenue) with what it actually proves to a skeptical owner who thinks in POs, not impressions.
- **Verified proof required:** No, methodology, not a results claim.

## 12. Timeline, honestly

- **Slots:** kicker, body
- **Brief field:** industry-brief.md timeline content, matched to BigOrange's own published pattern (early movement month one, compounding around month six)
- **Voice guidance:** State it straight, including the lag. Trust-building slide, undersell, don't oversell.
- **Verified proof required:** Yes if a specific number of days/months is stated, must match BigOrange's own published timeline, never an invented faster one.

## 13. The next right step

- **Slots:** kicker, body, CTA
- **Brief field:** none
- **Voice guidance:** Literal CTA, see the plan, book the walkthrough, check your AI visibility. Help, not a funnel ambush. One playful line max.
- **Verified proof required:** No.

## 14. Close / contact

- **Slots:** title, body (contact info, logos/badges)
- **Brief field:** "Proof patterns that work", named third-party recognition (Best in Cincy, Expertise Cincinnati, HubSpot/Google Partner)
- **Voice guidance:** Minimal copy, let badges and contact info close the deck.
- **Verified proof required:** Yes for any badge/award claim, confirm currently accurate before shipping.
