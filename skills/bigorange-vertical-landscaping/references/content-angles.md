# Landscaping content angles

15 angles grouped by the buyer job they serve. Each carries a one-line hook
in BigOrange voice and the proof it needs before it ships. An angle with no
proof on hand ships as a planning/informational piece, not a proof-dependent one. Don't force a stat or case study that doesn't exist yet.

## Job: "Convince me you're not going to flake on me"

1. **Before/after project spotlight**
   Hook: "Here's the yard on a Monday. Here's the yard three weeks later."
   Proof needed: real job photos, homeowner permission on file.

2. **Meet the crew**
   Hook: "The people showing up in your driveway, by name."
   Proof needed: real crew names/photos; no stock imagery.

3. **Review roundup, unedited**
   Hook: "We didn't write these. Our customers did."
   Proof needed: real, current reviews with permission to quote/screenshot.

## Job: "Tell me what this is actually going to cost"

4. **What landscaping actually costs (a real range)**
   Hook: "The honest number nobody puts on their homepage."
   Proof needed: the business's own pricing ranges, confirmed by the
   client. Never a guessed or industry-average figure presented as this
   business's price.

5. **What drives the price up or down**
   Hook: "Same yard, different quote. Here's why."
   Proof needed: none required beyond the client's own confirmed pricing
   logic; informational.

6. **Financing/payment options explainer** (design-build/hardscape)
   Hook: "The patio you want, the payment plan that doesn't hurt."
   Proof needed: confirmed financing terms from the client; never imply
   terms that aren't actually offered.

## Job: "Help me plan around the seasons"

7. **Spring cleanup checklist**
   Hook: "What your yard's been waiting all winter to tell you."
   Proof needed: none; informational, seasonal-timing anchored.

8. **When to book before you're on a waitlist**
   Hook: "Spring books up faster than you'd think. Here's when to call."
   Proof needed: the client's own historical booking pattern if available;
   otherwise keep the claim general ("spring fills up fast"), not a
   specific date pulled from nowhere.

9. **Fall is not the off-season**
   Hook: "The best time to plan next year's patio is this year's fall."
   Proof needed: none; informational/planning-stage.

10. **Winterizing your irrigation, done right**
    Hook: "Skip this and spring costs you a burst pipe, not a green lawn."
    Proof needed: none required for the how-to; any specific repair-cost
    figure needs a client-confirmed number, not an invented one.

## Job: "Show me you know what you're doing"

11. **The mistake we see homeowners make (a DIY-gone-wrong angle)**
    Hook: "The $40 fix that turned into a $400 fix."
    Proof needed: a real, anonymized example from the client's own job
    history if one exists; otherwise keep it general and don't attach a
    specific dollar figure that isn't real.

12. **How we choose plants/materials for [region]'s climate**
    Hook: "Not every plant that looks good at the nursery survives here."
    Proof needed: none; expertise-demonstration content, grounded in the
    client's actual regional knowledge.

13. **A hardscape material comparison (pavers vs. stamped concrete, etc.)**
    Hook: "Both look great on day one. Here's what year five looks like."
    Proof needed: none required for the general comparison; specific
    durability/cost claims should trace to the client's own experience or a
    citable source, not a guess.

## Job: "Answer my dumb-feeling question"

14. **"Do I actually need a landscaper for this?"**
    Hook: "Some of this is a Saturday project. Some of it really isn't."
    Proof needed: none; honesty-forward, trust-building informational piece.

15. **What to ask before you hire anyone (including us)**
    Hook: "The three questions that separate a real crew from a truck with a
    magnet sign."
    Proof needed: none required; frames licensing/insurance questions
    generally; see `industry-brief.md` compliance cautions before naming
    any specific license type as universally required, since that varies by
    state and service.
