# Landscaping industry pitch: deck narrative

A 12-to-14-slide arc for `bigorange-client-decks`'s `industry-pitch.json`
recipe. Each slide lists its slots, the `industry-brief.md` section that
fills each one, BigOrange-voice copy guidance, and whether the slide is
allowed to run without verified proof.

Slot names: `title`, `kicker`, `body`, `stat`, `proof`. Not every slide uses
every slot; use only the slots listed for that slide.

## 1. Cover

- Slots: `title`, `kicker`
- Source: prospect name/logo (from the client-decks brief, not this file) +
  brief's one-line summary as tonal anchor.
- Voice: `title` names the business and the promise plainly, no metaphor
  yet. `kicker` can carry one citrus/growth touch ("let's get your pipeline
  growing") since it's the cover's one playful line.
- Proof required: no.

## 2. The worry, named

- Slots: `kicker`, `body`
- Source: brief → "Who the buyer is" closing line: pipeline full, people
  busy, not babysitting the phone.
- Voice: open with the reader's actual worry as a question or a flat
  statement, not an agency observation. Lead with "you," not "landscaping
  companies."
- Proof required: no.

## 3. Who you are (buyer-type mirror)

- Slots: `title`, `body`
- Source: brief → "Who the buyer is" section; pick the one paragraph matching this
  prospect's actual shape (design-build / maintenance / hardscape /
  commercial). Do not paste all four.
- Voice: mirror their business back plainly so they feel seen, not
  categorized.
- Proof required: no.

## 4. How your customer actually finds you

- Slots: `title`, `body`, `stat`
- Source: brief → "Their customer's journey."
- Stat slot: the 81%-online-research figure or the BrightLocal review
  stats. Cite BigOrange or BrightLocal by name in the stat caption, with
  the date seen.
- Voice: plain physical language: searching at night, worried about a dead
  lawn, checking reviews on the porch.
- Proof required: yes. Stat must carry its source and date; do not run this
  slide with a number that isn't in `industry-brief.md`.

## 5. What's usually broken (diagnostic)

- Slots: `title`, `body`
- Source: brief → "What is usually broken" items 1-4 (GBP, website, reviews,
  photo proof); pick the 2-3 most relevant to the actual audit findings for
  this prospect, don't dump the full list generically.
- Voice: guide-not-hero framing: "here's what we usually find," not "here's
  what's wrong with you."
- Proof required: no, but items claimed should trace to an actual audit
  finding for this prospect where one exists, not a generic guess.

## 6. What's usually broken (the leak)

- Slots: `title`, `body`, `stat`
- Source: brief → "What is usually broken" items 5-6 (quoting/follow-up
  leak, no measurement).
- Voice: make the cost concrete: a lead that goes cold, a job that goes to
  the competitor who called back first.
- Proof required: no stat is mandatory here, but if a specific response-time
  number is used it must be sourced or explicitly labeled as an industry
  pattern, not this prospect's measured number, unless it is.

## 7. The fix, in one sentence

- Slots: `title`, `body`
- Source: synthesis of brief's "What is usually broken" into BigOrange's own
  service framing (site, GBP, reviews, content, measurement); restate in
  plain verbs: find, fix, show, check, build.
- Voice: state what will be built, checked, measured, or improved. No magic
  language.
- Proof required: no.

## 8. Proof: real work, real numbers

- Slots: `title`, `proof`, `stat`
- Source: brief → "Proof patterns that work" section, named case study pattern.
- Voice: let the number and the name do the talking; minimal adjectives.
- Proof required: **yes, hard gate.** This slide may not run with a
  fabricated or generic case study. If BigOrange has no verified landscaping
  case study for this pitch, replace this slide's content with an honest
  placeholder ("case study to be added once we've worked together") rather
  than inventing one; see SKILL.md fail-closed rule.

## 9. Proof: what your customers will see (photo/review system)

- Slots: `title`, `body`, `proof`
- Source: brief → "Proof patterns that work" section, before/after pairs, review
  count/recency.
- Voice: describe the system (text-the-homeowner-that-night review ask,
  photo capture built into the job checklist) in plain verbs.
- Proof required: no fabricated numbers; may reference the BrightLocal stats
  from the brief as the "why this matters" backing, cited.

## 10. Objections we hear (pick 1-2)

- Slots: `title`, `body`
- Source: brief → "Objections and answers" section; select the 1-2 most likely for
  this prospect's stated situation (e.g. "referral-only" for an established
  firm, "tried it before" for a burned prospect).
- Voice: acknowledge before answering; don't argue with the reader.
- Proof required: no.

## 11. How we'll measure it

- Slots: `title`, `body`
- Source: brief → "KPIs and measurement ladder" section; present as the 5-rung
  ladder, or the 2-3 rungs most relevant to this prospect's buyer type
  (retention rung only applies to maintenance/commercial).
- Voice: "show our work" language: this is where BigOrange earns trust
  with specificity over promises.
- Proof required: no fabricated numbers; ladder structure itself doesn't
  need a citation; it's a framework not a claim.

## 12. Seasonality / timing (why now)

- Slots: `title`, `body`, `stat`
- Source: brief → "Seasonality and market context."
- Voice: make the calendar concrete: "spring cleanup bookings fill by
  [month]," tie the pitch timing to the season the prospect is actually in.
- Proof required: any market-size or seasonal-curve number must carry its
  source and date, and the `unverified_precision` figures in the brief must
  be flagged as approximate if used, never stated as exact.

## 13. Compliance/trust note (optional, hardscape/commercial-heavy decks)

- Slots: `title`, `body`
- Source: brief → "Compliance cautions" section; only include when the prospect's
  work touches licensing-sensitive services (irrigation backflow, pesticide
  application) or commercial/HOA photo permissions.
- Voice: frame as "we'll get this right with you," not a legal warning.
- Proof required: no.

## 14. Next right step

- Slots: `title`, `body`
- Source: BigOrange's own CTA pattern ("book appointment," "see the site,"
  "read the post") pattern, not from this brief; pull from client-decks' standard
  closing convention.
- Voice: make the CTA feel like help, one literal next action, no funnel
  language.
- Proof required: no.

## Slide-count note

Slide 13 is conditional. Decks that skip it land at 13 slides total
(1-12, 14 renumbered to 13); decks that include it run the full 14. Never
pad to hit 14 with a slide that has nothing to say.
