# SmartCare tiers

SmartCare is Align's post-go-live service line. Its tiers are named three
different ways across documents that have already gone to clients.

## The conflict

| Source | Tier names |
|---|---|
| Partner introduction deck, August 2026 | Stabilize · Optimize · Optimize Plus |
| Phased proposal deck, February 2026 | Stabilize · Optimize · Thrive |
| Marketing voice reference | Stabilize · Essentials · Accelerate · Transform |

Only the first word agrees. A prospect who sees two of these has grounds to ask
which one they are buying, and a consultant answering that question is guessing.

## Ruling until the brand owner decides

The **partner introduction deck** wins, because it is the most recent
client-facing document and it defines each tier by what it covers rather than by
name alone. The table below is what this skill renders.

| Tier | When it applies | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Stabilize | First months after go-live | Close out what the first payrolls surface, resolve configuration gaps, document the system as built, and get your administrators self-sufficient. |
| Optimize | Steady-state operation | Adds monthly performance and adoption reviews, support ticket review, a key events calendar, and quarterly business reviews. |
| Optimize Plus | Added capacity, not just coverage | Adds managed services, where our team runs payroll, HRIS, or WFM operations alongside yours, plus one to three year platform planning. |

Every SmartCare engagement includes a dedicated executive sponsor, project
manager, and advisor, available Monday through Friday. Engagements are
hours-based and move between tiers as the client matures.

## To change this

Edit the table above. The build script reads it rather than carrying the tiers
in code, so a decision here reaches every deck without a code change.

When the brand owner rules, update the marketing voice reference in
`alignhcm-brand-system` to match, or the conflict simply moves rather than
resolving.
