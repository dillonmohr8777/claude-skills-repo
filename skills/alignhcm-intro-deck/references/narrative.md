# Why the slide order is what it is

The introduction deck is an argument, not a capabilities list. Each section
earns the right to the next one. Reordering it is the most common way a
generated deck stops working.

## The argument

1. **Who Is Align HCM** establishes standing. Facts only, no claims about the
   prospect. A reader decides here whether to keep listening.

2. **The partner who understands both sides** is the credential that matters
   for a migration: Align has lived on both the vendor side and the
   implementation side.

3. **What we heard from your team** comes before anything about methodology.
   This is the pivot. Until the prospect sees their own words, everything after
   it reads as a pitch. After it, the same content reads as a response.

4. **Why implementations fail** names the failure patterns before claiming to
   solve them. Most prospects buying an implementation partner have been through
   a bad one. Naming the pattern they lived through does more than any claim.

5. **Depth in their sector** turns the claim into evidence, in their world
   rather than in general.

6. **How we implement** is the methodology. It lands only after 4 and 5, because
   by then it reads as the answer to a named problem rather than as process for
   its own sake.

7. **Governance and cadence** answers the question the prospect has not asked
   out loud: what happens when it goes wrong.

8. **Change management** addresses the actual failure mode. Projects fail on
   adoption, not configuration.

9. **SmartCare** is where the value of the investment is realised or lost, and
   it is also the commercial expansion. It belongs after the implementation
   story, not inside it.

10. **Why organizations like them choose Align** closes by returning to their
    situation specifically.

## What not to do

- Do not move "what we heard" later to get to the methodology faster. That
  inverts the argument and the deck becomes generic.
- Do not merge the failure-pattern slide into governance. The pattern has to
  land before the counter.
- Do not add slides between 3 and 4. That gap is the strongest moment.
- Do not lead with SmartCare. It reads as an upsell before trust is established.
