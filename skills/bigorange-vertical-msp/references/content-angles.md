# MSP content angles

15 angles grouped by buyer job. Each has a one-line hook in BigOrange voice and the proof it needs before it can ship. Hooks are drafts, treat the specifics (numbers, names) as placeholders unless already sourced in `industry-brief.md`; never fill a placeholder with an invented stat.

## Job: "Show me you understand my actual problem"

1. **Hook:** Your website gets traffic and still doesn't ring the phone. Here's why.
   **Proof needed:** A real before/after example of a client fix, or the general industry pattern (BigOrange hub page) if no client-specific one is cleared to publish.

2. **Hook:** Running a great MSP is hard enough. Marketing shouldn't be one more system you have to babysit.
   **Proof needed:** None required, this is voice/positioning, not a claim.

3. **Hook:** "We do IT so you don't have to" is what every MSP site says. Here's what actually gets a prospect to call.
   **Proof needed:** Reference to real differentiation patterns from client proof (industry-brief.md, "Proof patterns that work").

## Job: "Prove this isn't just an agency pitch"

4. **Hook:** 71% of MSPs say winning new customers is their hardest problem right now. You're not behind, everyone's stuck at the same wall.
   **Proof needed:** Yes, cite Kaseya 2026 State of the MSP Report, dated, linked.

5. **Hook:** A Seattle MSP's traffic jumped 50 percent and leads tripled in four months. Here's what changed on their site.
   **Proof needed:** Yes, this is a named BigOrange case; must trace to source_ref before publishing as a standalone piece.

6. **Hook:** The gap between MSPs who can prove their value and the ones who can't just doubled in a year.
   **Proof needed:** Yes, cite Kaseya's 10% to 19% value-demonstration stat, dated.

## Job: "Help me understand what's actually broken on my site"

7. **Hook:** Five things missing from most MSP websites (and none of them are a redesign).
   **Proof needed:** No stat required, diagnostic checklist grounded in "What is usually broken" section.

8. **Hook:** Your contact form isn't a lead funnel. It's a dead end for anyone who isn't ready to call yet.
   **Proof needed:** No.

9. **Hook:** If your last five closed clients can't be traced to a channel, your marketing budget is a guess.
   **Proof needed:** No, grounded in the measurement-ladder gap, not an external stat.

## Job: "Help me decide whether to switch or stay"

10. **Hook:** You tried SEO once and it didn't work. Here's the difference between three months and the real six-month runway.
    **Proof needed:** Yes if citing the 60-90 day domain authority lag, must match BigOrange's own published timeline language.

11. **Hook:** Referrals built your MSP. Here's why they stop scaling past a certain size.
    **Proof needed:** No, logical argument, not a stat claim.

12. **Hook:** Questions to ask any marketing agency before you sign a 12-month contract.
    **Proof needed:** No.

## Job: "Help me understand the AI search shift"

13. **Hook:** Ask ChatGPT who the best MSP in your city is. If your name doesn't come up, here's what that costs you.
    **Proof needed:** No stat required for the hook itself, but any specific AI-visibility claim made about a prospect must be a live, checkable demo, not an invented result.

14. **Hook:** Answer engine optimization isn't a buzzword, it's the difference between showing up in an AI answer and not existing.
    **Proof needed:** No.

## Job: "Show me the people, not just the pitch"

15. **Hook:** Meet the team that got a Seattle MSP found. (Behind-the-scenes / team credit post, social-voice format.)
    **Proof needed:** Yes if referencing the specific case, must trace to source_ref; if used purely as team-credit without result specifics, no stat required.
