# MSP pitch deck narrative

Slide-by-slide arc for an MSP-vertical pitch deck. Slot names match what `bigorange-client-decks/recipes/industry-pitch.json` expects to fill from `references/industry-brief.md` (this file) and this narrative file. Each slide names its slots, the brief field feeding each slot, BigOrange-voice guidance for the copy, and whether the slide requires a verified proof point before it can ship.

General voice reminder: one playful line per section max, no em dashes, plain verbs, always a human consequence next to any technical claim. See `bigorange-brand-system` voice contract for the full rulebook.

## 1. Title

- **Slots:** title, kicker
- **Brief field:** none (client name/logo comes from the deck request, not the brief)
- **Voice guidance:** Kicker names the reader's world in one phrase, "for MSPs who are tired of being invisible" style, not a company tagline. Title is short, plain, no jargon.
- **Verified proof required:** No.

## 2. The problem, in their words

- **Slots:** kicker, body
- **Brief field:** "Who the buyer is", what they're worried about, in their words
- **Voice guidance:** Open with the reader's frustration, not BigOrange's pitch. Use the physical, ordinary language rule ("dragging rocks uphill," "already-full plate"). One line only, then move on.
- **Verified proof required:** No.

## 3. You're not imagining it, this is industry-wide

- **Slots:** kicker, stat, proof
- **Brief field:** "Seasonality and market context", the 71% customer-acquisition stat
- **Voice guidance:** Let the number do the work. One short sentence of context, cite Kaseya and the date. No editorializing beyond a plain "you're not alone."
- **Verified proof required:** Yes, this is an external, cited stat (Kaseya 2026 State of the MSP Report). Must carry source + date on the slide itself, not just in speaker notes.

## 4. What's actually broken

- **Slots:** kicker, body (list of 3-4 items)
- **Brief field:** "What is usually broken"
- **Voice guidance:** Name the specific failure, not a category, "a contact form and a phone number, nothing for someone who isn't ready to call" beats "poor lead capture." Keep each item to one line.
- **Verified proof required:** No, this is diagnostic framing, not a claim about the specific prospect (confirm with an actual site audit before naming their site's specific gaps).

## 5. Why this costs more than it looks like

- **Slots:** kicker, body, stat
- **Brief field:** "Seasonality and market context", the value-demonstration stat (10% to 19%)
- **Voice guidance:** Connect the abstract stat to a concrete cost: a lost renewal, a prospect who went with a competitor because the competitor's site had proof and this one didn't.
- **Verified proof required:** Yes, cited stat, source and date on slide.

## 6. Meet the guide, not the hero

- **Slots:** kicker, body
- **Brief field:** none (BigOrange positioning, from brand-voice.md)
- **Voice guidance:** BigOrange as guide, prospect as hero, StoryBrand framing, one sentence. "We've done this for MSPs before" plainly stated, not oversold.
- **Verified proof required:** No.

## 7. How buyers actually research an MSP today

- **Slots:** kicker, body, stat
- **Brief field:** "Their customer's journey"
- **Voice guidance:** Explain the rep-free research behavior in plain terms, "your next client is reading your reviews before they ever call you." Cite Gartner stat plainly.
- **Verified proof required:** Yes, Gartner stat (61% rep-free preference), source and date visible.

## 8. What we'll build: the plan

- **Slots:** title, body (numbered steps)
- **Brief field:** none (BigOrange's own process, content marketing, SEO site, AI search optimization, StoryBrand messaging, CRM/HubSpot, social, as service names, not the brief's diagnostic content)
- **Voice guidance:** Plain verbs only: find, fix, show, check, build. Each step is one clause. This is the "blueprint" section, concrete, not aspirational.
- **Verified proof required:** No, this is BigOrange's offer, not an external claim.

## 9. Proof it works

- **Slots:** kicker, proof (2-3 named results), stat
- **Brief field:** "Proof patterns that work"
- **Voice guidance:** Named results only, client name or description, specific number, specific timeframe. "Website traffic jumped 50 percent and leads tripled in four months" is the model sentence.
- **Verified proof required:** Yes, absolutely. Every item on this slide must trace to a source_ref in the brief or the deck request. Never invent or round up a case study. If only unverified anecdotes exist for this specific prospect's niche, say so and use BigOrange's own general proof instead, never fabricate a substitute.

## 10. Answering the objection in the room

- **Slots:** kicker, body
- **Brief field:** "Objections and answers" (pick the 1-2 most relevant to this prospect)
- **Voice guidance:** Say the objection out loud before answering it, "you tried SEO before and it didn't work, so let's talk about why." Disarms rather than dodges.
- **Verified proof required:** Only if the answer cites a stat (e.g. the 60-90 day domain authority lag), then yes, source and date.

## 11. What we'll measure

- **Slots:** kicker, body (the KPI ladder, condensed to 4-5 lines)
- **Brief field:** "KPIs and measurement ladder"
- **Voice guidance:** "Here's how you'll know it's working" framing, plain, checkable, no vague promises. Pair each KPI with what it proves.
- **Verified proof required:** No, this is a methodology slide, not a results claim.

## 12. Timeline, honestly

- **Slots:** kicker, body
- **Brief field:** industry-brief.md's process/timeline content (early movement month one, compounding by month six)
- **Voice guidance:** State the real timeline, including the lag. This is a trust-building slide, undersell rather than oversell, per brand voice rule 9 (do not promise magic).
- **Verified proof required:** Yes if a specific number of days/months is stated, must match BigOrange's own published timeline language, not an invented faster number.

## 13. The next right step

- **Slots:** kicker, body, CTA
- **Brief field:** none
- **Voice guidance:** Literal CTA per brand voice rule (see the site, join the session, book the call), make it feel like help, not a funnel ambush. One playful line max, then the plain ask.
- **Verified proof required:** No.

## 14. Close / contact

- **Slots:** title, body (contact info, logos/badges)
- **Brief field:** "Proof patterns that work", third-party badges (HubSpot Partner, Google Partner, StoryBrand Certified Guide)
- **Voice guidance:** Minimal copy. Let the badges and contact info stand alone.
- **Verified proof required:** Yes for any badge/award claim, must be currently accurate, not stale.
