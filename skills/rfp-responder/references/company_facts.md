## Implementation Methodology

Align follows a proven five-phase implementation approach:

1. **Project Readiness / Planning** — Welcome call, readiness meetings, system access, meeting cadence, kickoff preparation
2. **Analysis & Design** — Requirements gathering with client SMEs; Align SMEs conduct 2+ meetings per week to define business processes and rules; deliverable is a documented system design
3. **Configuration & Build** — Configuration built collaboratively; internal QA; knowledge transfer; integration build
4. **Acceptance & Parallel Testing** — Unit testing (Align-led), UAT1 (client-led, Align-supported), UAT2 (client-led with minimal Align support); parallel payroll testing twice
5. **Go-Live & Support Transition** — Production support; post-live stabilization; project wrap-up; handoff to SmartCare or UKG support

**Project management tools used:**
- Smartsheets — project plans, RAID log, dashboards
- Mavenlink — PSA software for resource management, project financials, portfolio health monitoring
- UKG Community / UKG Launch — requirements gathering, SFTP, service requests
- Open Project — collaborative test case tracking
- Raven Intelligence — client reviews
- Microsoft Teams — scheduled and ad-hoc communication

**Key methodology principles:**
- Early and consistent client involvement throughout
- Weekly status calls + weekly PM-to-PM meetings + monthly executive check-ins
- Clear RACI model with defined Align and Client responsibilities at each phase
- Change management guidance, train-the-trainer, and end user training included
- Formal scope change management process with change orders when needed

## Standard Boilerplate Answers

### Describe your organization
Align HCM is a Human Capital Management implementation and managed services firm founded in 2018 and headquartered in St. Petersburg, FL, with offices in Toronto, ON. We have 60+ employees across the US and Canada and have completed over 300 HCM engagements for 200+ customers. We specialize in mid-market and enterprise organizations across services, distribution, manufacturing, retail, and non-profit sectors. Our supported platforms include UKG, Dayforce, Paylocity, HiBob, Workday, and ADP.

### How long have you been in business?
Align HCM was founded in January 2018.

### Where are your resources located?
All Align HCM resources are located in the United States or Canada. We are 100% onshore. Standard working hours are 8 AM to 6 PM Eastern Time.

### How do you ensure quality?
Align takes a five-pillar approach to total project quality: (1) Hire better — using candidate assessment technology to identify top performers; (2) Train better — through our industry-leading Align Academy program; (3) Prepare better — via a rigorous client readiness program before kickoff; (4) Monitor better — using Mavenlink for real-time portfolio health and Raven Intel for continuous client satisfaction feedback; (5) Maintain better — through our SmartCare post-live managed services offering.

### What is your on-time delivery rate?
Approximately 80–85% of Align projects are completed on time relative to the original proposed timeline. When delays occur, they are primarily driven by client readiness factors rather than Align delivery.

### Do you have experience with unionized workforces?
Yes. Align recommends that each union/CBA agreement have its own configuration to allow for flexibility and ease of future maintenance. SmartCare clients benefit from proactive support when CBAs are renegotiated.

### Have you taken over a failed or stalled implementation?
Yes. As recently as Q4 2023, Align successfully took over three stalled projects from another implementation partner. Our approach is to begin with a joint meeting with the platform vendor and the client to align on project status and goals before creating a remediation plan.

### What is your approach to change management?
We provide train-the-trainer services as part of any new module rollout, along with guidance on how and when to communicate changes to impacted employees. We proactively set expectations, engage change champions early, and emphasize consistent communication with all affected stakeholders.

### How do you handle scope changes?
Scope changes are a normal part of any project. Our PM and the client PM first discuss the change; Align's executive sponsor then reviews and determines if additional charges apply. A formal change order is issued for signature before any scope change is executed.
