# Align HCM voice and copy rules

Consolidated from `brand-guidelines.md` (both copies) and the voice sections of
the legacy `alignhcm-brand` and `alignhcm-carousel-video` skills.

---

## Audience

Decision-makers and implementation leaders who **already understand** UKG,
Workday, Dayforce, HiBob, and payroll operations. Do not explain what an HRIS is.
Do not define go-live. Assume fluency and write to a peer.

## Tone

Professional, consultative, expert-level. Never salesy. The posture is a senior
practitioner explaining what actually happens after go-live, not a vendor
pitching. Credibility comes from specificity, not enthusiasm.

---

## Hard rules

These are absolute. They come from `brand-guidelines.md` and are stated there as
non-negotiable.

1. **No em dashes anywhere. Ever.** Use periods, commas, colons, or restructure
   the sentence.
2. **Use contractions.** "You'll" not "you will".
3. **Bullet character only.** Never a dash as a list marker.

## Typographic rules

- Letter-spacing is never left at default in designed assets. Range `-0.01em` to
  `-0.045em` for display type; wide positive tracking (`0.16em`–`0.34em`) for
  caps eyebrows and labels.
- Headlines run 8–15 words.
- One accent word per headline carries the underline animation on animated
  surfaces.

---

## Recurring phrases

These are established. Reuse them rather than inventing synonyms:

- "Go-live"
- "Proactive"
- "No queue, no chatbot"
- "Stabilize → optimize → accelerate → transform" (maturity ladder)
- "Post-go-live support"
- "Ongoing expert access"
- "Platform optimization"

## SmartCare messaging

SmartCare is Align HCM's post-implementation service line.

**Core positioning:** ongoing expert access, platform optimization, post-go-live
support.

**Maturity ladder:** Stabilize → Essentials → Accelerate → Transform.

**Service structure:** Maintenance tiers (Essentials, Accelerate, Transform),
Managed Services (Payroll, HRIS, WFM), and Strategic Advisory.

**Named assets:** free assessment workshop, FTE ROI calculator,
Post-Implementation Maturity Assessment.

**Case studies:** Troon, Peco Foods, Burnco/Hammerstone, Humber Meadows,
Cavvy Energy, GTAA.

**Stats that appear in approved copy:** 82%, 2–4 FTEs, $200K–$400K,
$500K/month MRR.

## When copy is missing

If an asset needs something not covered here, such as exact email subject lines,
blog outlines, or specific ad copy, say so explicitly. Offer to write net-new and mark
it as net-new. Do not fabricate approved-sounding copy.
